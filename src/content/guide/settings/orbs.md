---
title: Orbs
order: 4
---

Two bodies are rarely at an exact angle. The **orb** is how far from exact an aspect can be and still count.

Each aspect has its own orb: 8° for a conjunction or opposition, 7° for a square or trine, 4° for a sextile, and 2° for the minor aspects.

- **Standard** uses those orbs.
- **Tight** makes each three-quarters as wide, for fewer, stronger aspects.
- **Wide** makes each a quarter wider, for more.
