---
title: House system
order: 1
---

The houses divide the sky into twelve parts, each tied to an area of life. A house system is the rule for drawing the lines between them. Astrologers disagree on the best one, so Aster offers several:

- **Placidus**: the most widely used. It divides the sky by time: how long each degree takes to climb from the horizon to the midheaven.
- **Koch**: also divides by time, but measured from the midheaven's degree.
- **Whole Sign**: the oldest. Each sign is one house, starting with the ascendant's sign.
- **Equal**: twelve houses of 30° each, starting at the ascendant.
- **Porphyry**: divides each quarter between the angles into three equal parts.
- **Regiomontanus** and **Campanus**: divide the sky itself into equal parts, in two different ways.

Placidus and Koch can't be drawn inside the polar circles. There, Aster uses Porphyry instead and says so.
