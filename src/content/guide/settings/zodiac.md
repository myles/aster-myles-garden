The zodiac is the ring of twelve signs that positions are measured along.

- **Tropical**: starts Aries where the Sun is at the March equinox. It follows the seasons, and is the default in Western astrology.
- **Sidereal**: lines the signs up with the constellations behind them. Used in Vedic astrology.

The two drift apart about 1° every 72 years and are now about 24° apart, so most placements move back a sign in sidereal.

An **ayanamsa** is the exact size of that gap. Traditions measure it slightly differently, so Aster offers several; **Lahiri** is the most common.
