---
title: Aspects
order: 3
---

An aspect is a meaningful angle between two bodies in a chart.

**Major only** finds the five classic aspects: conjunction (0°), sextile (60°), square (90°), trine (120°) and opposition (180°).

**With minor** adds four quieter ones: semi-sextile (30°), semi-square (45°), sesquiquadrate (135°) and quincunx (150°). Minor aspects are drawn as dashed lines on the wheel.
