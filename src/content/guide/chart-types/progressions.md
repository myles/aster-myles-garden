title: Progressions
order: 4
---

Secondary progressions move the chart on one day for each year of life: the sky 30 days after birth stands for age 30.

The progressed chart sits outside your birth chart. **Progressions to your chart** lists the aspects its bodies make to your natal ones, within 1°. Progressed bodies move slowly, so even 1° can last a year or two.
