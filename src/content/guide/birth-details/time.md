---
title: Time
order: 2
---

The local clock time where you were born, as written on a birth certificate. Aster works out the time zone and daylight saving for that place and day, so enter the time as the clock showed it.

The time sets the **ascendant**, the **midheaven** and the **houses**. These turn through all twelve signs each day, about one sign every two hours, so a few minutes can move them.

Don't know the time? Tick **I don't know the time**. The chart is cast for noon, without houses or angles, and notes any body that changed sign during the day.
