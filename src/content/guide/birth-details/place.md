title: Place
order: 3
---

The town or city where you were born. Its latitude and longitude set the houses, and its time zone sets how your birth time is read.

Places are searched in your browser, from a list drawn from [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org/). If yours isn't listed, choose **Not listed? Enter coordinates** and type its latitude and longitude. Aster suggests the time zone of the nearest listed place; check it's right.
