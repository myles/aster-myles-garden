Where each body was at birth.

- **Position** is the sign and the degree within it, from 0° to 29°. "14° 32′ Leo" means 14 degrees and 32 minutes into Leo.
- **℞** means the body was retrograde: from Earth, it seemed to move backwards.
- **House** is the house it falls in.

The **ascendant** is the point of the zodiac rising on the eastern horizon. The **midheaven** is the point at the top of the sky, due south in the northern hemisphere. They're the chart's two main angles, and depend on the birth time.

Besides the planets, the table shows three points: the **North and South Nodes**, where the Moon's path crosses the Sun's, and **Lilith**, the point of the Moon's orbit farthest from Earth.
