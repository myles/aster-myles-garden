---
title: The wheel
order: 1
---

The wheel is a map of the sky at the moment of birth, seen from the place of birth.

- The **outer ring** holds the twelve signs.
- Each **body** sits at its position along the ring, marked with its glyph and degree.
- The **lines across the middle** divide the houses, numbered 1 to 12 anticlockwise from the left.
- The four **angles** are labelled: **AC**, the ascendant, rising in the east; **DC**, the descendant, setting in the west; **MC**, the midheaven, at the top of the sky; and **IC**, opposite it.
- The **lines between bodies** in the centre are aspects. Minor aspects are dashed.

**Describe the wheel**, under it, gives the same in words.
